# Store listing text

Google Play `Store listing` copy for the languages the app ships. Play's limits:
**title 30 characters**, **short description 80**, **full description 4000**.
Paste each block into Play Console → *Grow → Store listing → Manage translations →
Add language*, picking the matching locale.

Titles say *unofficial* in the reader's own language: WikiTree's name and logo are
theirs, and a listing that looks official invites both confusion and a takedown
request. If you would rather keep the brackets off, use plain `WikiTree Viewer`
(16 characters) and rely on the first line of the full description.

Locales match the app's six languages: `en-US`, `de-DE`, `es-ES`, `pt-BR`,
`fr-FR`, `sr-RS` (Serbian in Latin script). If you prefer European Portuguese,
change "compartilhar"/"aparelho" to "partilhar"/"telemóvel" and name the locale
`pt-PT`.

WikiTree Viewer is an unofficial, read-only viewer for family trees published on
WikiTree. It is not affiliated with WikiTree.com, and all family data comes from

WHY WIKITREE IS WORTH YOUR TIME

## Before you publish

Play asks a few things this text does not cover, and they matter for review:

- **Privacy policy URL** (required by Play *and* by Apple, even for an app that
  collects nothing): `docs/privacy/index.html` in this repository is ready for
  GitHub Pages — Settings → Pages → *Deploy from a branch* → `main` / `/docs` —
  which serves it at `https://under3415.github.io/wikitree-viewer/privacy/`. Fill
  in the two highlighted placeholders (developer name and contact e-mail) first:
  a policy without a working contact address is rejected, and the e-mail in it is
  the address Play shows on your listing.

- **Data safety form**: the app collects nothing but anonymous crash diagnostics.
  Declare "Crash logs" as collected, not shared, for app functionality, and state
  that it is not linked to the user. Everything else is "no". The exact answers,
  for both Play and Apple, are in `store/data-safety.md`.
- **Ads**: no. **In-app purchases**: no, and the listing must not suggest otherwise.
- **App access**: no login, so answer "all functionality is available without
  special access" and give the reviewers nothing to sign in with.
- **Trademark**: keep the word *unofficial* visible in the title or the first line.
  WikiTree's terms do not grant the use of their name for an app that looks
  official, and the emblem in `art/` is derived from their logo.
- **Screenshots**: the tree of `Kennedy-96` with a couple of generations open shows
  the app better than any of the text above. Capture at 1080×1920, at least four,
  and use the same six languages for the screenshot captions if you want the
  full "localised" badge.
